# Privacy Policy - QuoteFlow

**Last updated: March 25, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect or store any personal data. The only information processed is the text provided by the user to be converted into a video quote.

## 2. How We Use Information
The provided text is used exclusively to generate video content as requested by the user. 

## 3. Data Storage
We do not store any user-provided text or generated video content permanently. All data is processed and discarded immediately after the video generation process is complete.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Our integration with TikTok uses their official APIs to allow users to share their content. Please refer to TikTok's privacy policy for information regarding their data practices.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions, feel free to reach out via our GitHub repository.
